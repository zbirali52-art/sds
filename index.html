How Strong Determination Sitting is Done

In Pali, Adhiṭṭhāna signifies unshakeable resolution. Sitting for 60 minutes without shifting a single muscle reprograms your relationship with physical aversion, mental restlessness, and sensation.

01
👁️
Eyes Firmly Closed
Visual input constitutes 80% of brain stimulation. Closing your eyes turns the lens inward. Even if a sudden sound or shadow arises, do not break the seal of your eyelids.
02
🤲
Hands & Arms Motionless
Rest your hands gently in your lap or on your knees. When an itch strikes your face or nose, do not lift a finger. Observe the sensation peak, burn, and dissolve.
03
🧘
Legs & Posture Locked
No uncrossing legs, no slouching, no micro-shifting to relieve pressure. Anchor your seat like a granite mountain. The posture set at minute 0 remains at minute 60.
🛡️ The Rule of Movement: "Honest Time Out"

Strong determination sitting requires absolute honesty. If pain becomes so intense that you shift your posture, scratch your skin, or open your eyes, you must immediately press "I Moved (Call Time Out)".

Calling Time Out is not a defeat. It records the exact minute and second your stillness held (e.g., 42 minutes). By logging the sensation that prompted the move, both Zabir and Soham can track their progressive threshold expansion over weeks.

🌊 The "Urge Surfing" Technique (Surviving the Fire)

Between minute 35 and 50, intense sensations will arise—heat, throbbing, knife-like burning in hips or knees, violent urges to fidget. The brain panics and sends urgent signals to move.

  • Deconstruct the Sensation: Instead of labeling it "terrible pain", zoom in like a scientist. Is it temperature (hot/cold)? Pressure (heavy/light)? Vibration (tingling/throbbing)?
  • Watch the Anicca (Impermanence): Sensations rise in waves like an ocean swell. They peak, stay intense, and inevitably shift or break. Ride the crest of the wave with your breath.
  • Relax the Face & Jaw: The body unconsciously clamps the jaw, forehead, and shoulders when pain arises. Consciously melt these muscles.
🪑 Optimal Posture Alignments

Burmese Style

Both feet flat on cushion/mat in front of each other, not overlapping. Easiest on ankles and knees for 60m sits.

Half / Full Lotus

One or both feet resting on opposite thighs. Highly stable triangular base. Requires flexible hips.

Seiza (Kneeling Bench)

Kneeling with hips supported on a zafu or angled meditation bench. Keeps the spine naturally upright.
